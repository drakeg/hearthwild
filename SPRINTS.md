# Sprints

**Vision:** an original, friendly online fantasy RPG that a small group of friends can host themselves or play together on a hosted world: classes, quests, dungeons, raids, professions and guilds, built openly under the AGPL.

Sprints are one week. Each has a goal, committed stories from the [backlog](BACKLOG.md), and ends with a release, a review and a retrospective. Detail for each sprint (acceptance criteria, progress log, review, retro) is in [`docs/agile/sprints/`](docs/agile/sprints/); the process is in [`docs/agile/README.md`](docs/agile/README.md).
