## Descriptions

**One line:** An original online fantasy RPG for you and your friends: host your own world, or join theirs.

**Short (about 50 words):** Hearthwild is an online fantasy role-playing game made for groups of friends. Pick one of four classes, quest across nine zones to level 30, and take on nine dungeons, two ten-player raids and a colossus that roams the world. Host it on your own computer in one click, or join a friend's.

**Long (about 150 words):** Hearthwild is an original online fantasy RPG built for the people you already play with. Make a Warrior, Rogue, Warlock or Druid of the Dawnward or the Mossbound, and set out from Wayfarer's Camp in the Stillwood. Nine zones stretch out from there, from amber downs and frozen vales to the lava fields and fallen stars of the Outer Ring.

Group up for nine dungeons, each one your group's own copy, with bosses whose attacks you dodge and whose spells you interrupt. At level 28, gather ten friends for the Sundered Spire or the Molten Crown. When Gharrowmaw, the Wandering Colossus, wakes, the whole world hears.

Between adventures, there are herbalism, alchemy, mining and blacksmithing, plus guilds, mail, face-to-face trading and 25 achievements. The desktop app runs its own server, so anyone can host a world for friends. Hearthwild is free software.

## Features

- Four classes with their own abilities, talents and Rank II upgrades.
- Nine zones, 46 quests, towns, caravans, riding and waystones.
- Nine dungeons and two ten-player raids, with telegraphed boss fights and Epic loot; a Dungeon Finder to build groups.
- A roaming world boss announced to everyone online.
- Four professions, guilds with ranks, mail, trading, faction chat, and 25 achievements with titles.
- One-click hosting from the desktop app, with a Server Manager for invites, moderation and backups; Docker for home servers.
- A free demo: the Stillwood and the Hollow Crypt, up to level 6.
