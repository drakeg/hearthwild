## 0.18.0 — Raids: The Sundered Spire

**The first raid, for ten adventurers at level 30.** The Sundered Spire stands in the far north-east of Starfall Reach. Inside, three bosses wait in turn, each with its own fight and its own Epics.

- **Grimward the Gatekeeper** stomps the ground around him (*Warden’s Stomp*), brings down *Falling Masonry* on four of you at once, and calls Spire wardens to hold the gate.
- **Seraphel the Lightbinder** rains *Lightfall* on five of you, channels *Binding Light* (interrupt it!), and erupts in a *Prism Burst*. At 40% her light-bound acolytes join the fight.
- **Nythera, Heart of the Void** opens *Void Rifts* under the raid, pulls everything in with *Collapse Inward*, and channels *Unmaking*. She calls void spawn at half health, and she enrages after seven minutes.
- **Raid groups:** a group leader can type **/raid** to turn a group into a raid of up to ten. Raids need a group, and level 28 or higher to enter. Boss health and damage scale with the number of raiders.
- **Raid loot:** each boss drops one of its own three Epics for every raider, and they're stronger than any dungeon's. Bosses also drop 150 gold each.
- **Quest:** Archon Maelis at the Spire's door gives a quest to close the Heart of the Void.
- **The Dungeon Finder fills raids:** queue for the Spire, and the Finder builds a raid of two tanks, two healers and six damage dealers. After three minutes in the queue, five with a tank and a healer is enough. Finder raids get the Finder satchel too.
- **Under the hood:** dungeons can now hold several bosses, each fighting, resetting and dropping loot on its own. Only the final boss clears the instance and completes its quest.
- The death message now says "the entrance" instead of "the crypt entrance" in every dungeon.
- A hundred and ten checks, including new tests for the raid's bosses and loot, raid groups, entry rules, separate boss fights, clearing, and Finder raids.

## 0.16.0 — The Dungeon Finder

With nine dungeons across the world, there's now a Dungeon Finder to put groups together.

- **Open it with O** (or the *Dungeon Finder* menu button). Pick your role — **Tank** (warriors), **Healer** (druids) or **Damage** (everyone) — and tick the dungeons you want. You can queue for any dungeon from one level below its first creatures.
- **Queue alone or as a group.** A group leader queues the whole group, and each member keeps the role they picked. A group can't bring two tanks or two healers.
- **Matching:** the Finder builds groups of one tank, one healer and three damage dealers from your faction, oldest in the queue first. After 90 seconds in the queue, a tank, a healer and one more is enough.
- **Ready check:** when a group is found, everyone sees who's in it and has 40 seconds to accept. Anyone who declines or doesn't answer leaves the queue; everyone else keeps their place.
- **Straight in:** when all accept, you're formed into a group led by the tank and carried into a fresh copy of the dungeon. Walking out through the portal, or leaving the group, takes you back to where you queued. Released spirits still return to the dungeon door so they can run back in.
- **Finder satchel:** killing the boss with a Finder group pays each member a bonus of gold and experience that grows with the dungeon's level.
- While you wait, a small banner under the minimap shows your time in the queue.
- Names of adventurers standing right beside the camera now fade out instead of filling the screen.
- A hundred and three checks, including new tests for matching, the ready check, declining and timing out, smaller groups after a long wait, group queues, role and level rules, the satchel, and returning home.

## 0.14.0 — The Outer Ring

**The world doubles in size, to about 4 km across.** Beyond the first four zones lies the Outer Ring: four new zones for levels 20–30, each with its own town, people, quests, creatures, gear and dungeon.

The forge-town of **Cinderhold** stands at the end of the road from Cragmoor Hold. Cinder hounds, Emberbacks, Cinderhold reavers and Ashbound zealots roam the waste.

- **Starfall Reach** (north-east, levels 27–30): high meadows tinged violet, strewn with glowing starstone crystals and the Starglass Tarn, around **Starfall Rest**. Star lynxes, Voidmaws, Astral seekers and Starfall sentinels roam the Reach.

- **Four new dungeons**, each with its own look and a boss fight with two or three mechanics:
  - **The Cinderforge** (levels 22–24): **Forgelord Ashkar** unleashes *Molten Burst* and *Slag Rain*, and calls forge hounds.

  - **Tomb of the Sun-King** (levels 26–28): **Sun-King Azhar** casts *Solar Flare*, *Sandstorm* and the long *Judgement of the Sun*.
  - **The Shattered Observatory** (levels 29–31): **Astromancer Velia** calls *Starfall*, opens a *Gravity Well* and channels *Collapse*.

- **21 new quests:** four quest givers in each new town (zone hunts, collection quests, chains), courier runs that carry you from Cragmoor Hold around the Outer Ring to Starfall Rest, and a boss quest at each dungeon door.

## 0.13.0 — Room to Grow

Groundwork so the world can keep getting bigger without slowing down.

- **Zone maps.** **M** now opens a map of the zone you're in, with its town, houses, lakes, dungeon (and its levels) and quest givers. Switch to **World** for the whole world, and click any zone there to open its map. Zone maps are painted in finer detail than the world map, and the minimap uses the sharper painting too.
- **Smoother maps.** Maps are painted a few milliseconds at a time in the background, so they never stall the game.
- **Smaller, faster updates.** The server now sends each player only what's around them: creatures, herbs and loot within 200 m, and other adventurers within 160 m. Before, other adventurers vanished beyond 40 m. Content that never changes (shop, quests, classes, abilities, people) is sent once instead of several times a second, so updates are 50–85% smaller.
- **Sleeping creatures.** Creatures with nobody within 230 m stop wandering until someone arrives. Fights, effects over time and respawns carry on as before. The server's work no longer grows with the size of the world, only with where players are.
- Ninety checks, including new tests for nearby-only updates, content sent once, sleeping creatures and zone map views.

## 0.11.0 — Roads and Riches

- **Gear for every zone.** Each new zone drops its own Uncommon and Rare weapon, chest piece and charm, 24 new items in all, from the *Harrowford militia sword* to the *Eye of the Tarn*. Gear now has a level requirement, and creatures drop gear close to their own level, so every few levels brings an upgrade. Zone gear sells to merchants but isn’t in their shops.
- **Abilities up to level 20.** Every class learns four more abilities at levels 6, 10, 14 and 18, on action bar keys **9, 0, - and =**, each with new artwork. They bring three new effects:
  - **Shields** absorb damage: *Shield wall*, *Bone ward*, *Oakhide*, *Smoke veil*.
  - **Damage over time:** *Wither* and *Bleeding gash* keep hurting after the hit, even if you fall.
  - **Stuns** freeze a creature and break its spell: *Thunder slam*, *Paralysing hex*, *Thornsnare*, *Hilt strike*. Bosses are immune.
  - And big finishers: *Cleaving sweep*, *Titan’s strike*, *Cataclysm*, *Sunlance*, *Verdant tide*, *Heartseeker*.
- **Four quest givers in every town, with 16 quests in all.** As well as each town’s zone hunt, there are collection quests (seed grain, bog-oak idols, wolf pelts, runestones…), hunts for particular creatures, and quest chains that unlock as you go. Courier quests send you down the road to the next town to deliver a letter, report or map. Quests you can’t take yet (an earlier step, or your level) show why.
- **Riding.** Stablemasters at Wayfarer’s Camp and Harrowford teach riding from level 10 for 50 gold. Press **U** (or the Mount button) to ride a great amber fox at 60% more speed. You dismount when you fight, take a hit or go underground.
- **Caravans.** A caravan master at the camp and in every town will carry you along the roads to any town you’ve already discovered, for a small fare. Creatures leave caravan passengers alone.
- **Waystone.** Talk to any provisioner to make their town your home. Press **H** (or the Waystone button) to return home after a 10-second channel. Moving or taking a hit breaks it, and it recharges over 15 minutes.
- Eighty-two checks, including six new tests for gear tiers, abilities and their effects, quest chains and deliveries, riding, caravans and the waystone.

## 0.10.2 — Facing forward

- **Characters no longer run sideways.** The character models were turned a quarter turn away from the direction they moved, so your hero (and NPCs and humanoid enemies) ran with their body side-on. Running forward now shows your character’s back, strafing leans the body toward the direction of travel, and enemies face you as they charge.

## 0.10.1 — Random loot and no Documents prompt

- **Loot is random again.** Each kill rolls its own drop: often just coin, sometimes an Uncommon item, now and then a Rare, and occasionally a draught, bread or water. Gold now scales with the creature’s level. (Before, the item was picked by your total kill count, so after five kills every drop was the same.)
- **The Mac app no longer asks to access your Documents folder.** It only uses its own save folder, or a development copy in `~/Sources`. It never looks in Documents, Desktop or Downloads, even if an older version saved a location there.
- Seventy-six checks, including a new test that kills vary their loot.

## 0.9.0 — Guilds

- **Found a guild.** Registrar Corwin Hale, beside the campfire at Wayfarer’s Camp, sells a guild charter for 10 gold. Name your guild, ask two adventurers of your faction to sign it (click them and choose *Ask to sign charter*), then bring it back to found the guild. You become its Guild Master and the signers become Members.
- **Ranks:** Guild Master, Officer, Member and Initiate.
  - Officers and the Guild Master invite new members (who start as Initiates), promote, demote and remove lower ranks, and set the message of the day.
  - Only the Guild Master can pass on leadership (*Make GM*) or disband the guild.
  - If the Guild Master leaves, the highest-ranked, longest-serving member takes over automatically. When the last member leaves, the guild disbands.
- **Guild window (J, or the Guild button):** roster with level, class, rank, location and online status; the message of the day; invitations; and member management.
- **Guild chat** with `/g`, and **officer chat** with `/o`. Both are also in the chat channel menu. Commands: `/ginvite`, `/gpromote`, `/gdemote`, `/gkick`, `/gleader`, `/gmotd`, `/gquit`, `/groster`.
- Your guild’s name appears in angle brackets under your name for everyone nearby. Members see the message of the day and a note when guildmates come online.
- Guilds are saved with the world and survive restarts. Server hosts can change the number of charter signatures with `HEARTHWILD_CHARTER_SIGNATURES`.
- Builds for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux.
- Seventy checks.

## 0.8.0 — Groups and the Hollow Crypt

- **Groups of up to five.** Click another adventurer and choose *Invite to group*, or type `/invite Name`. The invitee gets an Accept/Decline popup. Party frames appear under your portrait. The leader can `/kick` and `/promote`; anyone can `/leave`. Party chat with `/p`. Groups are same-faction only.
- **Shared credit.** Group members within 40 paces share kill experience (with a 25% group bonus) and quest credit, and each gets their own loot roll.
- **The Hollow Crypt, an instanced dungeon.** Scout Haldren, beside the sealed door in Amberwatch Ruins, gives the quest *Ashes beneath Amberwatch*. Each group (or solo adventurer) gets its own copy of the crypt: an entry hall, passages, a ruined chapel, an ossuary and the Warden's Sanctum, built from the CC0 KayKit Dungeon pack with flickering torchlight.
  - Linked trash packs: pull one and its friends come too.
  - **Boss: Vorlath the Ashen Warden.** Move out of the red Ashfall circles, interrupt *Soul Drain* (it heals him), and deal with the acolytes he calls at half health. He enrages after four minutes. A wipe resets the fight. He drops Epic loot: the Ashbound greatblade, Mantle of the Ashen Warden or Ember of Vorlath.
  - Health scales with group size, so the crypt can be explored solo, but the boss is meant for a group.
  - Interrupt abilities: Crushing Blow, Soul Scorch, Feral Rake and Venom Edge also interrupt enemy casts. The target frame shows a cast bar marked "interrupt!".
  - The minimap shows a dungeon map inside. Dying inside returns your spirit to the crypt entrance.

- Sixty-four checks.
