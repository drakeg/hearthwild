# Original ability icon art

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-27. The selected production atlas is `public/art/abilities.png` in this project; it is used directly as a 4 × 4 CSS sprite sheet. No third-party game artwork is included. Rows map to Warrior, Warlock, Druid and Rogue; columns map to abilities 1–4. Gear slots currently reuse representative atlas cells, rather than unique item art.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original game ability icon atlas for Hearthwild. Create a square 4 by 4 grid of 16 equal square painted fantasy RPG ability icons, edge-to-edge perfectly aligned cells, no gaps, no outer margin, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no borders. Rich hand-painted fantasy game art, bold readable silhouettes, dramatic light and painterly texture. NOT copied from any game. Every individual cell has its own dark colored square background. Row 1 warrior: silver sword sweeping red arc; massive golden hammer impact; raised shield with healing gold light; cracked earth orange shockwave. Row 2 warlock: violet flaming projectile; purple burning skull; spectral teal siphoning hand; exploding purple energy ring. Row 3 druid: green thorn projectile; luminous green healing leaf; claw rake in amber green; blooming restorative white green flower. Row 4 rogue: curved dagger slash yellow; dripping venom green dagger; small healing potion; paired silver daggers amid smoky blue swirl. Each subject centered with safe inset within its 1/16 cell. All 16 cells equally sized. No UI mockup; pure production sprite atlas.
