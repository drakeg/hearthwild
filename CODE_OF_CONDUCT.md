# Code of Conduct

Hearthwild is a friendly place to make a game together. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions, or in game worlds run for the project — is expected to follow this code.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and say what would make it better.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or intimidation of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or slurs.
- Sexual content or attention aimed at anyone.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or other players' games.

## Enforcement

Problems can be reported privately to the project owner, @drakeg, through GitHub; reports are kept confidential. The owner may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block anyone who breaks this code, temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
